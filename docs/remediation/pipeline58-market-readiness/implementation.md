# Pipeline 58: gap-aware market-data admission

Pipeline 58 exposed two distinct failures: CLBK received no historical data from IB, while HLT's valid November 9 bars were discarded because November 4 was missing. The former whole-response coverage rule and full-universe readiness gate prevented otherwise usable companies from continuing.

New normal admissions explicitly freeze `CONTINUE_KNOWN_DATA_GAPS_V1`. Known company-specific no-data outcomes remain unavailable and produce null, ineligible technical diagnostics. Ready companies continue, every requested company remains in scope, and the final outcome records partial coverage. Certification admissions remain strict. Existing admissions without a version marker retain their original behavior; current defaults never reinterpret old anchors or fetch plans.

Response policy `ib-daily-gap-aware-v4` separates historical coverage from bar integrity. It retains validated, completed, reviewed bars, records every missing required session and reports a genuine partial acquisition. Contract, OHLCV, duplicate, calendar and upper-bound protections remain enforced. Required benchmark and sector dependencies retain strict response policies, and their failures cannot disappear outside the requested-company readiness list. Bootstrap/application failures remain blocking.

Migration `0095_ib_bar_admission_receipts` adds append-only acceptance receipts. Each receipt binds exact bar addresses, data/revision fingerprints, contract, request scope, owner and calculation context. Cache writes, receipt and item result share an atomic transaction. Frozen readers authorize exact per-bar versions across original and repair receipts; they do not infer acceptance from a FAILED/PARTIAL/SUCCESS status. Previously captured manifests continue to validate against their original receipt/version after later repairs or revisions. The original nine-field legacy acquisition authority remains compatible.

Current-session availability and historical completeness remain separate. HLT can have an admitted November 9 bar while calculations requiring its incomplete historical window remain ineligible. Existing producer readiness and consumer permissions enforce that distinction. CLBK cannot receive a stale numeric technical score merely because old cached history exists. Fundamental source evidence is preserved.

The progress page reports unavailable companies and historical gaps separately, with returned and available endpoints. **Start new run with data gaps** explicitly creates an isolated recovery upload and new pipeline for the source pipeline's entire company population. It admits a new cutoff/configuration, retains exact source-row lineage, coalesces repeated requests and leaves the original run unchanged. It is the recovery path for legacy pipeline 58, whose rejected responses have no acceptance receipts.

**Review missing history** and **Fetch missing days** use a fingerprinted, receipt-backed obligation manifest. The resulting bounded child acquisition retains the parent context, contract, cutoff and configuration; it does not depend on a latest-date shortcut. TRADES can repair the older interval directly; current-ended ADJUSTED_LAST remains limited by the original session expiry. The worker inherits the exact execution configuration. Direct handler calls without that delivery, stale manifests, changed temporal fields, altered plans and mismatched child authority fail before provider requests. Existing results are not automatically recalculated.

The exact IB wording `HMDS query returned no data` is recognized narrowly as instrument-specific unavailability. Explicit pacing/transient messages retain retry behavior, and unrelated/unknown error 162 messages are not made permanent.

Focused validation includes deterministic provider responses, real PostgreSQL migrations and immutable SQL guards, rollback injection, exact repair/version unions, frozen worker delivery, legacy manifest compatibility, preservation of the full recovery population and original source fingerprints, null technical evidence and Combined withholding. Runtime and implementation evidence is retained in the workspace's `docs/remediation/pipeline58-market-readiness` directory. Operational actions and endpoints are documented in `docs/operations/market_data_recovery.md`.
