# SwingLens Remediation Master Decisions

Date: 2026-08-02

Decision classes:

- `ENGINEERING_SAFETY_DEFAULT`: conservative engineering decision can be made now.
- `PRODUCT_DECISION`: product semantics or user-facing scope must be chosen.
- `LEGAL_OR_PROVIDER_DECISION`: provider terms, licensing, or compliance semantics must be chosen.
- `DEFERRED_ENHANCEMENT`: not required to unblock S0/S1 release safety if explicitly deferred.
